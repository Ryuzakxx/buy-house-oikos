The voice interview

The voice interview uses your browser's speech recognition. Your browser's provider converts the audio to text (in Chrome this is done on Google's servers, in Safari by Apple); the site receives only the text. If you prefer not to use it, the guided chat asks the same questions.

Your rights

You can ask to access, correct or delete your data, to restrict or object to its processing, and to receive it in a portable format (articles 15 to 22 GDPR). You can withdraw any consent at any time, without affecting what was done before. You can also complain to the Italian supervisory authority, the Garante per la protezione dei dati personali (garanteprivacy.it).

This prototype

In the current demo version of the site, nothing you enter is sent to a server: interview answers, briefs, bookings and messages are stored only in your browser's local storage. You can delete them by clearing this site's data in your browser.
