--- faq Q: Can foreigners buy property in Lake Como? A: Yes, on the same rules as anywhere in Italy: EU citizens on the same terms as Italians, others subject to the reciprocity condition, which the notary checks. See the buying process. Q: Which airport is most convenient? A: It depends on where in Lake Como you buy: Milan Malpensa, Milan Linate and Bergamo (MXP, LIN, BGY) are the main options.
