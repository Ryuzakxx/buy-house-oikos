After the interview

  1. You review your brief. Every criterion appears under Must have, Nice to have or Deal breaker. Move it, remove it or change the answer.
  2. You send it to a consultant. With your contact details and the consents you choose. Nothing leaves your device before this step.
  3. We search. First among the homes Oikos holds and partner agencies' listings. If nothing fits yet, we search through partner agencies, colleagues and other sources for you.
