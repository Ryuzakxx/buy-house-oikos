Key facts

  • Airports: Genoa, plus Nice for the western Riviera and Pisa for the eastern end (GOA, NCE, PSA).

  • Typical properties: apartments in harbour towns, villas on terraced hillsides, rustici among olive terraces.

--- faq Q: Can foreigners buy property in Liguria? A: Yes, on the same rules as anywhere in Italy: EU citizens on the same terms as Italians, others subject to the reciprocity condition, which the notary checks. See the buying process. Q: Which airport is most convenient? A: It depends on where in Liguria you buy: Genoa, plus Nice for the western Riviera and Pisa for the eastern end (GOA, NCE, PSA) are the main options.
