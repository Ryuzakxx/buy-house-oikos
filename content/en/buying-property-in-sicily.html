--- faq Q: Can foreigners buy property in Sicily? A: Yes, on the same rules as anywhere in Italy: EU citizens on the same terms as Italians, others subject to the reciprocity condition, which the notary checks. See the buying process. Q: Which airport is most convenient? A: It depends on where in Sicily you buy: Catania, Palermo, Trapani and Comiso (CTA, PMO, TPS, CIY) are the main options.
