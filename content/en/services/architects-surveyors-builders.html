Who does what

Geometra (surveyor)
Cadastral updates, planning applications for smaller works, measurements, and often site management on modest projects.
Architect
Design, permits for larger or protected projects, site management.
Structural engineer
Structural and seismic assessment, calculations for structural work.
Builder (impresa edile)
Carries out the work under a contract with you, supervised by the site manager (direttore dei lavori).

Before you buy: the feasibility check

If your plans depend on work (adding a pool, opening a terrace, converting an outbuilding), ask a professional to check feasibility before the preliminary contract: what the local plan and landscape rules allow, which permits are needed and roughly how long they take. In protected areas a pool or an extension can need landscape approval in addition to the building permit.

Permits, in brief

Main types of building permission
TypeTypically used for
Free works (edilizia libera)Ordinary maintenance and some minor works
CILAExtraordinary maintenance that doesn't touch the structure
SCIAWorks affecting the structure or the façades, many renovations
Permesso di costruireNew buildings, extensions, major changes

Which one applies depends on the work and on the municipality's rules; a landscape authorisation may be needed on top.

--- faq Q: Can I get a cost estimate before buying? A: Yes, and you should if the work is significant. A professional can give a rough budget after a site visit; a detailed quote needs a project. Q: Are there tax incentives for renovation? A: Italy has run several renovation tax credits, and their rules and rates change often. Ask a tax adviser which ones apply when you do the work, and whether you can use them if you don't pay tax in Italy.
