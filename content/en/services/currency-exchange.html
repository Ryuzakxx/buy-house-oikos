Traceable payments

The deed must state how the price was paid. Payments go by bank transfer or banker's draft (assegno circolare) and must be traceable; cash isn't an option for the price. Leave time for the funds to arrive: international transfers and compliance checks can take several days.

Where the money comes from

Banks, notaries and agents are required to check the origin of funds. Keep documents ready that show it: the sale of a property, savings, an inheritance, a loan.

What we do

We tell you the date and amount of each payment as soon as they're known, introduce you to currency providers and check that the money reaches the right account before each deadline.

--- faq Q: Should I fix the exchange rate when I sign the preliminary contract? A: That's when the amount and the date are known, so it's when a forward contract becomes possible. Whether it's worth it depends on how much risk you're comfortable with; a currency provider can explain the costs.
