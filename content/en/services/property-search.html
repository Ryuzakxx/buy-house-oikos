Where we search

Your brief goes first through the homes Oikos holds directly, then through the listings of our partner agencies, which share their properties with us so our buyers can see them. Then we ask colleagues in the areas you've chosen, who often know of homes before they're advertised, and we check public portals and judicial auctions when they're relevant.

If nothing in our database matches your brief, the search doesn't stop: we'll search through partner agencies, colleagues and other sources for you, and tell you what we've found and how close it comes.

How the shortlist is built

Every home we consider is compared with your brief:

  • a deal breaker that isn't met rules the home out, however good the rest is;
  • each must have counts three times as much as a nice to have;
  • each nice to have adds a little.

Next to each home on your shortlist you'll see which criteria it meets, which it misses and which we still have to check on site, such as whether a pool could be built. The score helps us sort; what you visit is your decision.

When your brief changes

It usually does after the first proposals or visits. When you tell us why a home didn't work ("too far from the shops", "too much renovation"), we update the brief with you, so the next proposals reflect it.

What we need from you

  • A brief, from the guided chat, the voice interview or a video call.
  • Your timeline, and whether you'll need a mortgage: both change which homes are worth visiting.
  • Honest feedback on what we send you, including "no".
