Codice fiscale
A 16-character code that identifies a person to the Italian tax authority (Agenzia delle Entrate). It is derived from name, date and place of birth.

When you'll need it

  • To sign a purchase offer or a preliminary contract.
  • To register the preliminary contract and the deed.
  • To open an Italian bank account or apply for a mortgage.
  • To set up electricity, gas, water and internet.
  • To pay property taxes.

How to get it

  • At an Italian consulate in the country where you live, with your passport. Procedures vary by consulate, so check its website.
  • At an Agenzia delle Entrate office in Italy, with your passport, or your identity card if you're an EU citizen. It is usually issued on the spot.
  • Through someone you authorise, with a signed proxy and a copy of your document.

We fill in the request form with you (form AA4/8, for individuals) and book the appointment. Everyone whose name will be on the deed needs their own code.

--- faq Q: Does a codice fiscale make me tax resident in Italy? A: No. It's only an identification number. Having one doesn't make you tax resident and doesn't create tax obligations on its own. Q: How long does it take? A: At an Agenzia delle Entrate office it's usually issued the same day. At a consulate it depends on its workload, so ask early.
