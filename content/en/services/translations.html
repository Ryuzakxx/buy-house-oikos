What needs translating

  • The purchase offer and the preliminary contract, before you sign them.
  • Survey reports and documents from the checks.
  • The deed of sale: if you don't understand Italian, the notary must use an interpreter and attach a translation.
  • Documents from your own country that the notary or the bank needs: powers of attorney, marriage certificates, company documents.

Working translation or sworn translation?

To understand a contract, a careful working translation is enough. For documents an Italian authority must accept, you may need a sworn translation (traduzione asseverata, sworn before an Italian court or notary) or one certified by a consulate. We tell you which kind each document needs before you pay for it.

The interpreter at the deed

The interpreter takes an oath before the notary and translates the deed for you at the signing. We book one for your language in advance, together with the notary's date.
