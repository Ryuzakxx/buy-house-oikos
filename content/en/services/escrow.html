How it works

Italian law (Law 147/2013, as amended by Law 124/2017) lets either party ask the notary to deposit the price in a dedicated account. The notary releases it to the seller only after the deed has been registered in the property registers and nothing prejudicial, such as a mortgage, a seizure or a claim, has been registered between the deed and that moment. Sums in the dedicated account are kept separate from the notary's own assets.

When to ask for it

  • The seller has a mortgage that will be paid off from the price.
  • The seller is a company, or there are doubts about their finances.
  • You want certainty that nothing can be registered against the property in the days after the deed.

Deposits paid before the deed

Deposits paid with the offer or the preliminary contract usually go to the seller. To protect them, the preliminary contract can be signed before a notary and registered (trascrizione) in the property registers, which protects you against a later sale or mortgage by the seller. --- faq Q: Does the notary charge extra for holding the price? A: Ask whether it changes the fee and have it included in the written estimate before the deed. Q: Is the money safe if something happens to the notary? A: The law keeps sums in the dedicated account separate from the notary's personal assets, so they are not part of the notary's estate or reachable by the notary's creditors.
