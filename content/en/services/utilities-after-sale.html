Utility contracts

  • Electricity and gas. The existing contract is transferred to your name (voltura) or replaced with a new one, with meter readings taken on the day of the deed.
  • Water. Managed by the local water operator, which handles the transfer of the contract.
  • Internet. Fibre, fixed wireless or satellite in rural areas. If you'll work from the house, check coverage before you buy.
  • Waste tax (TARI). Register with the municipality within the deadline it sets.

Taxes after the purchase

IMU, the municipal property tax, is due on homes that are not your main residence, in two instalments with deadlines on 16 June and 16 December. We set up reminders with you or your accountant. See Italian property taxes.

The first year

  • Garden, pool and maintenance contacts for when you're away.
  • Insurance for the building and its contents.
  • Keyholding and checks after storms.
  • If you plan to let the house: registration with the region and the municipality, tourist tax, and the national identification code for short-term rentals (CIN).
