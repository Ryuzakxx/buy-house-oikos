A service built around the buyer

Property portals show what sellers want to sell. Buyers from abroad need something different: someone who starts from what they want, knows where to look beyond the listings, and stays with them through a purchase run in a language and a legal system they don't know yet.

So we start with your brief, written down and sorted into must haves, nice to haves and deal breakers. We search against it, explain every match, and adjust the brief with you after each proposal and visit.

Working with other agencies

No single agency has every home. Partner agencies share their listings with us so that our buyers can see them, and share part of their commission when one of our buyers buys one of their homes. We tell buyers when this applies. How the partnership works.

What you can expect from us

  • Every home we propose comes with the reasons it fits your brief, and what it misses.
  • We tell you who is paid by whom in every transaction.
  • We say when a question is outside our competence, and who you need instead: a notary, a lawyer, a tax adviser.
