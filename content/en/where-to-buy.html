Choosing an area

Most buyers start with a picture: a trullo among olive trees, a terrace over a lake, a house a short walk from the sea. The questions that narrow it down are practical ones, and they're the same ones our interview asks:

  • How far from an airport with flights to where you live?
  • Will you be there all year, or mostly in summer? Many coastal places are quiet in winter.
  • Do you want to walk to a café and a shop, or is a drive fine?
  • Are you ready to renovate? It decides between old stone houses and newer villas more than the region does.

If you're undecided between two areas, say so in your brief. We'll propose homes in both and the comparison usually settles it.
