Before you start

  • Voice input works in Chrome, Edge and Safari. In other browsers we'll take you to the guided chat with the same questions.
  • Your browser's speech service turns what you say into text. In Chrome this audio is processed by Google's servers; Safari uses Apple's. We only receive the text.
  • Your browser will ask permission to use the microphone.
  • You can switch to typing at any point. Answers given by voice are kept.
