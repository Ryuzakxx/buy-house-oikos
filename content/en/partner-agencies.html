How the partnership works

  1. You share your listings, through your Apimo account or a feed we agree on. There's no fee to list and no exclusivity.
  2. We match them to buyers' briefs. Your properties enter our matching alongside Oikos listings. They aren't published in a public catalogue: we show them only to buyers whose brief they fit, and only with your agreement.
  3. We introduce the buyer. We record which buyer was shown which property, and when, so the introduction is documented on both sides.
  4. At completion, you share the commission. When a buyer we introduced completes the purchase of one of your properties, you recognise us a share of your commission, indicatively 1% of the sale price. The exact terms are set in a written agreement before any introduction.

What you get

  • Buyers who already know what they want, with a written brief and a budget.
  • The language work done for you: we handle the buyer's questions, translations and the coordination with their lawyer or translator.
  • No cost unless a sale completes.

What we ask of you

  • Accurate listings, and prompt updates when a property goes under offer or is sold.
  • Availability for viewings, in person or by video.
  • The cadastral and planning documents when a buyer is ready to make an offer.
