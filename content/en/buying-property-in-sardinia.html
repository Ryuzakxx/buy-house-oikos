--- faq Q: Can foreigners buy property in Sardinia? A: Yes, on the same rules as anywhere in Italy: EU citizens on the same terms as Italians, others subject to the reciprocity condition, which the notary checks. See the buying process. Q: Which airport is most convenient? A: It depends on where in Sardinia you buy: Olbia, Cagliari and Alghero (OLB, CAG, AHO) are the main options.
