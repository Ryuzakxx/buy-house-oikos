Three questions that depend on your country

  • Can you buy? EU and EEA citizens buy on the same terms as Italians. Others need their country to allow Italians to buy there (the reciprocity condition), which the notary checks.
  • How long can you stay? Non-EU citizens can spend up to 90 days in any 180 in the Schengen area without a visa. Owning a home doesn't change that.
  • Where do you pay tax? Income from an Italian property is taxed in Italy; your country may tax it too, with relief under a double tax treaty.
